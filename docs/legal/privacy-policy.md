## 1. Summary

- The App primarily stores your data **on your device**.
- The App currently handles **health and wellness data**, including activity, location, sleep, hydration, workout, and mindfulness information.
- The App currently does **not** include user accounts, subscriptions, in-app purchases, advertising SDKs, or analytics SDKs in the codebase reviewed for this draft.

- Data may leave your device when:

  - map or replay features request map resources over the internet;

## 3. How We Collect Information

We collect information:

1. **Directly from you**, such as when you enter profile values, sleep notes, workout notes, or mindfulness reflections.
2. **From device sensors and services**, such as activity recognition, step sensors, and location services when you enable related features.
3. **From device state signals**, such as charging state, device interactivity state, and time-based scheduling used for sleep and reminder features.
4. **From your use of the App**, such as when you start or stop tracking, export a replay, dismiss notifications, or reset settings.

## 4. Permissions and Sensitive Data

The current Android app requests or uses the following sensitive or notable permissions/features:

- **Activity Recognition** for step tracking.
- **Precise and approximate location** for run/walk tracking.
- **Background location** if enabled by the user for tracking behavior while the app is not actively open.
- **Notifications** for foreground services, reminders, and alarms.
- **Exact alarms / scheduled alarms** for certain sleep-related reminders where supported.
- **Boot completed** so reminders/workers can be re-scheduled after device restart.

We only intend these permissions to support the App's wellness and tracking features. If a permission is denied, some features may be limited or unavailable.

## 5. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- create and maintain your activity and wellness history;
- personalize goals, units, reminders, bedtime/wake calculations, and summaries;
- run foreground services for step tracking and run/walk tracking;
- generate route maps, analytics, and replay exports for your runs;
- log, review, and manage sleep sessions, including manual entries and automatic heuristics;
- deliver reminders and notifications related to water, steps, sleep, and active tracking;
- troubleshoot app behavior, improve reliability, and respond to support or legal obligations.

We do **not** use the reviewed in-app data for targeted advertising or data brokerage.

## 6. Where Data Is Stored

Based on the codebase reviewed:

- Most app records are stored locally in the App's internal Room database and DataStore files on your device.
- Some profile fields are stored using Android protected storage mechanisms and app-internal files.
- Exported replay files are written to the App's cache directory before sharing.
- The App also allows Android backup/restore features at the manifest level, and the current backup rule files are not tightly scoped. As a result, some app data may be included in device backup or transfer workflows depending on Android version, device settings, and platform behavior.

We do not sell your personal data.

4. **Service providers.** If we later engage vendors for hosting, support, compliance, or operations, we may share data with them under appropriate contractual and legal safeguards.
5. **Legal or business events.** We may disclose data if required by law, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, security, users, or the App; or as part of a merger, financing, acquisition, or asset sale, subject to applicable law.
